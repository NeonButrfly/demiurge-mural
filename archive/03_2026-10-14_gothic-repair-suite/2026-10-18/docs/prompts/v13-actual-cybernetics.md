## User correction

> you didnt actually add cybernetics

V12 was rejected because copper edge accents and line work did not read as physical cybernetic machinery.

## Implemented requirement

- Start from the unchanged v11 composition rather than building on rejected v12.
- Add solid, visibly dimensional copper hardware: fitted plates, bolted ports, articulated joint collars, attached conduits, a segmented hooded gauntlet, demon harness and wing-root hardware, spear bracer/clamp, and smaller lower-body implants.
- Use the supplied copper artwork only for palette and mechanical vocabulary; do not paste, replace, or redraw its artwork into the destination.
- Keep faces and anatomy unchanged. Every modification must remain inside the exported v13 hardware mask.
- Keep the rest of v11 byte-identical and provide difference, out-of-mask, detail, and QA exports.

## Validation boundary

Automated checks establish dimensions, copper presence, artifact generation, and strict overlay confinement. They do not establish artistic acceptance; issue #14 remains open until the user approves the placement and styling.
