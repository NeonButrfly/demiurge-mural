Linked issue: [#2](https://github.com/NeonButrfly/demiurge-mural/issues/2)

“recreate a single 800x800 image with the border around the outside”

The output has a 790 × 790 mm artwork interior, a uniform low-Z outside border, no internal border, and remains within the 800 × 800 mm envelope.
